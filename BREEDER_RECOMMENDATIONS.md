## 🏆 Top Features Professional Breeders Need

### 1. **Waitlist & Application System** (CRITICAL)
**Why:** Manages buyer interest, screens applicants, prioritizes serious buyers

**Features Needed:**
- Puppy application form (detailed questionnaire)
- Waitlist management dashboard
- Application status tracking
- Auto-email notifications
- Priority ordering system
- Match buyers to puppies

**Industry Standard:**
- First-come, first-served OR
- Best-fit matching system
- Deposit required to secure spot

### 2. **Deposit & Payment System** (CRITICAL)
**Why:** Locks in serious buyers, reduces no-shows, streamlines transactions

**Features Needed:**
- Online deposit payment ($300-$500 typical)
- Balance payment tracking
- Refund policy automation
- Payment confirmation emails
- Integration with Stripe or PayPal

**Industry Best Practices:**
- Partial refundability ($150 of $300 refundable)
- Non-refundable after puppy selection
- Clear terms and conditions
- Automatic receipts

### 3. **Litter Management System** (HIGH PRIORITY)
**Why:** Keeps buyers informed, builds excitement, manages puppy assignments

**Features Needed:**
- Current litters page
- Upcoming planned breedings
- Puppy availability status (Available/Reserved/Sold)
- Birth announcements
- Weekly update posts with photos
- Puppy individual profiles
- Selection timeline

**Content to Track:**
- Birth date
- Number of puppies
- Male/female count
- Colors/markings
- Availability status
- Expected go-home date

### 4. **Analytics Dashboard** (HIGH PRIORITY)
**Why:** Understand visitor behavior, optimize marketing, track conversions

**Metrics to Track:**
- Total visitors (daily/weekly/monthly)
- New vs returning visitors
- Page views and popular pages
- Time on site
- Bounce rate
- Traffic sources (Google, Facebook, referrals)
- Geographic locations
- Device types (mobile/desktop)
- Contact form conversion rate
- Application form submissions

**Tools:**
- Google Analytics 4 (free, comprehensive)
- Firebase Analytics (already have Firebase)
- Custom dashboard for key metrics

### 5. **Admin Login Button** (EASY WIN)
**Why:** Quick access to dashboard without typing URL

**Implementation:**
- Small "Admin" link in footer
- Or icon in header (lock symbol)
- Protected behind authentication
- Hidden from general public

### 6. **Enhanced Parent Dog Profiles** (MEDIUM PRIORITY)
**Why:** Buyers want to know the bloodlines, health, temperament

**Information Needed:**
- Professional photos
- Registered name & call name
- Date of birth
- Height & weight
- Health clearances (hips, elbows, eyes, heart, genetic)
- Titles & achievements (show, performance)
- Temperament description
- Pedigree (3-5 generations)
- Offspring accomplishments

### 7. **Health Testing Documentation** (HIGH PRIORITY)
**Why:** Transparency builds trust, required by ethical breeding standards

**Content Needed:**
- OFA certifications (hips, elbows, cardiac, eyes)
- Genetic testing results
- Health guarantee policy
- What tests are done on all breeding dogs
- Links to health registries (OFA database)
- Explanation of what each test means

### 8. **Buyer Education Section** (MEDIUM PRIORITY)
**Why:** Educates buyers, reduces unqualified inquiries, sets expectations

**Topics to Cover:**
- Irish Wolfhound breed information
- "Is this breed right for you?"
- Cost of ownership (food, vet care, grooming)
- Space requirements
- Exercise needs
- Lifespan expectations
- Training recommendations
- Feeding guidelines
- Common health issues in breed

### 9. **Selection Process Documentation** (HIGH PRIORITY)
**Why:** Manages expectations, explains timeline, reduces anxiety

**Content to Include:**
- Timeline from birth to go-home (8-10 weeks)
- When waitlist is notified
- How puppies are matched to buyers
- What happens at pickup
- Puppy evaluation criteria
- What's included with puppy
- Spay/neuter requirements
- Contract overview

### 10. **Testimonials & References** (MEDIUM PRIORITY)
**Why:** Social proof, builds credibility, showcases happy families

**Content:**
- Buyer testimonials with photos
- Where puppies have gone (states/countries)
- Success stories
- Photos of grown dogs
- Reunion events
- Social media testimonials

## 💰 Payment Integration Options

### Option 1: Stripe (Recommended)
- **Pros:** Professional, widely trusted, low fees (2.9% + $0.30)
- **Cons:** Requires business verification
- **Best for:** Professional breeders, recurring use

### Option 2: PayPal
- **Pros:** Everyone has it, easy setup
- **Cons:** Higher fees (3.49% + $0.49), buyer protection disputes
- **Best for:** Quick setup, occasional use

### Option 3: Square
- **Pros:** No monthly fees, good for in-person
- **Cons:** Limited recurring payment features
- **Best for:** Hybrid online/in-person

### Option 4: Firebase + Stripe Extension
- **Pros:** Integrates with existing Firebase backend
- **Cons:** Technical setup required
- **Best for:** Your current setup (recommended)

## 📋 Application Form Questions (Industry Standard)

### Buyer Information
1. Full name
2. Email
3. Phone
4. Address (city, state)
5. Best time to contact

### Household Information
1. Do you own or rent your home?
2. If renting, does landlord allow large dogs?
3. Do you have a fenced yard? Size?
4. Who lives in your household? (adults, children, ages)
5. Does everyone agree to getting a puppy?

### Experience & Lifestyle
1. Have you owned dogs before? What breeds?
2. Have you owned a giant breed before?
3. Current pets? (species, ages, spayed/neutered)
4. Are current pets up to date on vaccinations?
5. How many hours will puppy be alone daily?
6. Where will puppy stay when alone?
7. Where will puppy sleep at night?

### Exercise & Training
1. How will you exercise your Irish Wolfhound?
2. Do you have experience training large dogs?
3. Are you willing to attend training classes?
4. What activities do you plan with your dog?

### Puppy Preferences
1. Male or female preference?
2. Color preference?
3. Intended purpose? (companion, show, sport)
4. Preferred temperament? (calm, playful, etc.)
5. When are you ready to bring puppy home?

### Commitment
1. Are you prepared for 7-10 year commitment?
2. Estimated annual budget for care? ($2000+/year)
3. Do you understand giant breed health issues?
4. Are you willing to spay/neuter per contract?
5. Will you return dog to breeder if unable to keep?

### References
1. Veterinarian name and contact
2. Personal reference (non-family)

## 💡 Waitlist Management Best Practices

### Deposit Structure Examples:
1. **Non-refundable deposit:** $500 (common)
2. **Partially refundable:** $300 total ($150 refundable before selection, $150 non-refundable)
3. **Tiered deposits:** $250 waitlist, $250 more after birth announcement

### Waitlist Priority Systems:

**Option A: First-Come, First-Served**
- Deposit date determines priority
- Fair and transparent
- Less flexibility for matching

**Option B: Best-Fit Matching**
- Breeder matches puppies to buyers
- Considers lifestyle, experience, preferences
- More work but better outcomes

**Option C: Hybrid**
- Deposit order within "groups"
- Priority for show homes vs companion homes
- Flexibility with fairness

### Communication Timeline:
1. **Application received:** Immediate auto-confirmation
2. **Application approved:** Within 48 hours
3. **Added to waitlist:** Confirmation email with position
4. **Breeding announced:** Email to all waitlist members
5. **Pregnancy confirmed:** Update with expected due date
6. **Birth announcement:** Photos, count, sexes
7. **Weekly updates:** Photos and developmental milestones
8. **Selection time:** Individual calls/emails in waitlist order
9. **Pickup reminder:** 1 week before, what to bring
10. **Post-pickup:** Follow-up at 1 week, 1 month, 1 year
