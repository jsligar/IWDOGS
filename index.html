About Our Kennel

Mount Olympus Irish Wolfhounds is a mother-daughter breeding team with over 21 years of experience raising and showing champion Irish Wolfhounds.

Our Philosophy

We have a true passion for the Irish Wolfhound breed and are dedicated to preserving and improving it. Our breeding program focuses on:

  • Health First: We prioritize genetic health and longevity above all else
  • Superior Genetics: Breeding only from health-tested, champion bloodlines
  • Excellent Temperament: Our dogs are known for their gentle, friendly nature
  • Show Quality: We compete in AKC and international dog shows

Recognition

We are proud to have been awarded WagWalking's Top Irish Wolfhound Breeder in 2017, recognizing our commitment to excellence in breeding practices.

Meet Our Irish Wolfhounds

Our dogs are members of our family and the foundation of our breeding program. Each has been carefully selected for health, temperament, and conformation to breed standards.

Champion Sire

Majestic and powerful, our champion sire represents the best of the breed with exceptional health clearances and show achievements.

Champion Dam

Graceful and athletic, our champion dam combines beauty with proven genetics and outstanding temperament.

Show Dog

Our show dogs excel in AKC and international competitions, demonstrating breed excellence and championship quality.
